Radiation now causes immediate damage and long term.

immediate :
 - Causes burn, bleeding and possibly skin peeling.
 - Causes organ damage



Adds a new "radiation exposure" affliction that goes up when exposed to radiation.
It very slowly goes down by itself, nothing can make it go down (tbd).
The higher rad exposure is, the higher the chance of cancer.
It can also slow down blood production.
It has a chance to kill the husk (certain at a certain exposure).

Two types of cancer:

Benign cancers do not spread and do not lower vitality. They do cause debuffs depending on the organ/limb.

Malignant cancers can spread and lower vitality. They do cause debuffs depending on the organ/limb.


Treatment :

Husk infection (80+%) slows down the progress of cancer.
Removal of limb/organ
Cyber organs and limbs cannot get cancer.
"Concentrated radiotoxin" slowly cures cancer but causes immediate damage.
